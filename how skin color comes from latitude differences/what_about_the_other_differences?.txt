BASED ON MY CURRENT LEVEL OF MIND:

- some of the common traits that we see between populations may have had distinct evolutionary benefits, and others may have
  just been by chance, and they ended up being neither advantageous nor disadvantageous, so they just stuck.

- a lot of this stuff is also social: how you talk, how you walk, what you dress. that's all social stuff, doesn't have
  any inherent biological roots (again, we're 99.9 percent the same)
