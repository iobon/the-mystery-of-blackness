my hands are ashy and black as coal
and there's soot under my nails.
carnation and mustard and tangerine scabs (still healing) yet mottle the surface
in a way reminiscent of rotting flesh.

my hands are itchy
i go to scratch them, and
chips and flakes of dry blood and plasma
fall to the earth in a pitiful flurry.

my hands, a crooked orchestra of color and texture
but the sun is still happy to kiss them
oh, how it lovingly kisses them
